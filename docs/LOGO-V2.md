## Промпт

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final logo symbol for a fictional warm neighborhood coffee shop named Крошка. Generate ONE clean original flat logo mark on a genuinely transparent background. Symbol only, absolutely no typography or letters. A plump little croissant forms a friendly compact crescent silhouette, subtly suggesting the rim of a coffee cup, with one small coffee-bean shaped cutout integrated into the center and exactly three small crumb dots above the right tip. One solid warm terracotta ink color #a74126, completely opaque solid shapes, a few negative-space cuts for the croissant folds. Rounded confident handmade curves, slightly imperfect independent bakery identity, minimal and graphic, cheerful but not childish. Bold and very readable at 40 pixels. Center the isolated mark, fill around 85 percent of a square canvas, tight clean silhouette. No shadows, no gradients, no outline box, no badge, no background color, no paper texture, no presentation mockup, no 3D, no watermark, no text.
